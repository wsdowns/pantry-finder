Text version of the flowchart
  1. A neighbor calls (844) 670-4047 from any phone. No app, no internet, no sign-up.
  2. Greeting and menu: “Welcome to Milledgeville food assistance. Press 1 for a food pantry, 2 for home delivery.”
  3. Press 1, food pantry. The caller keys a 5-digit ZIP (31061, 31062 or 31059). The line reads one pantry at a time, nearest first: name, distance, address, whether it is open now or when it opens, and its phone number.
  4. Press 2, home delivery. A short intro (mostly for seniors and people who can’t get out), then one program at a time: name and who qualifies, hours to call, and phone number. Five programs, in order.
  5. The same keys everywhere:
    • 1 connect me. For a pantry that is open, the call rings the pantry now. For a closed pantry, the caller is warned it may be a voicemail and the call rings longer so a message can be left. For delivery, the call rings the program’s office where there is a phone number; 2-1-1 and Meals on Wheels are read aloud only.
    • 2 say it again.
    • 3 next closest (farther out each time), or the next program.
    • 9 start over, back to the main menu.
  6. Behind the scenes: a Twilio toll-free number answers every call; our server checks the call really came from Twilio; open or closed is worked out at the moment of the call; a neural voice reads the answer, then connects the call.
